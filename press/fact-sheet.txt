IN ONE LINE
Carvalanche is a free one-thumb skiing game for iPhone: carve, flip and outrun the avalanche down an endless mountain, with a new Daily Challenge every day.

IN A PARAGRAPH
The slope cracks, and the avalanche is right behind you. Carvalanche is endless downhill skiing and snowboarding through fresh powder, seen from just behind the rider and played with one thumb: slide to carve, flick up to jump, flick down for speed. Every run is a new mountain that starts on a green circle and climbs through blue and black to double black, with glades, moguls, fallen trees, snow ramps for back flips and 720s, and narrow rocky chutes. Power-ups on the way down: a snowflake magnet, a super jump that clears the trees and a snow shield that smashes the next one. A numbered Daily Challenge, the same mountain for everyone, sets three goals a day (from collecting snowflakes to landing a double back flip), with three tries, three stars and a spoiler-free result to share. It's free, with no ads, no accounts and no data collected.
